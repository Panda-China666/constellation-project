## Overview

Study sequence modeling, architecture, training objectives and compute allocation in large language models. Topics connect attention, sparse computation, state-space models and non-autoregressive generation with training stability and inference efficiency.

**Duration**: 14 weeks · Graduate level  
**Prerequisites**: Deep learning, probabilistic sequence models, optimization and basic parallel computing.

## Weekly schedule

| Weeks | Research topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Sequence modeling, entropy rates, tokenization, discrete and continuous representations |
| 3–4 | Transformers, attention variants, positional representations, long-context mechanisms |
| 5–6 | MoE, sparse computation, state-space models, hybrid architectures |
| 7–8 | Pretraining objectives, data distributions, scaling laws, compute-optimal training |
| 9–10 | Training stability, optimizers, parameterization, gradient scales, cross-scale hyperparameter transfer |
| 11–12 | Beyond next-token prediction: multi-token prediction, discrete diffusion language models, parallel generation |
| 13 | Multimodal language modeling: unified generation of discrete and continuous tokens |
| 14 | Architecture research defense |

## Learning objectives

- Understand the central mathematical objects, algorithms and research methods.
- Identify assumptions, applicability limits and failure conditions.
- Connect literature review, theoretical analysis and reproducible experiments.

## Research assignment

Implement an architectural variant and compare it with strong baselines under fixed data, parameter or FLOP budgets. Report quality, throughput and memory trade-offs.

## Deliverables

Submit a research report with the necessary code or proofs. State the problem, related work, method, evidence and limitations.
