## Overview

Study parallelism, communication, memory and scheduling in foundation-model training and inference. Analyze system design under joint constraints on quality, throughput, latency, energy and cost.

**Duration**: 14 weeks · Graduate level  
**Prerequisites**: Distributed systems, parallel computing and deep learning; S1 is recommended.

## Weekly schedule

| Weeks | Research topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Distributed training: FSDP, ZeRO, tensor, pipeline and context parallelism |
| 3–4 | Expert parallelism, MoE all-to-all, communication–computation overlap |
| 5–6 | Distributed optimizers, activation checkpointing, asynchronous training, numerical consistency |
| 7–8 | Scaling infrastructure: topology, checkpoints, fault recovery, data pipelines |
| 9–10 | Inference: PagedAttention, KV caches, continuous batching, speculative decoding |
| 11–12 | Disaggregated prefill/decode, heterogeneous deployment, scheduling, tail latency |
| 13 | Training–inference co-design: objectives, architecture and inference cost |
| 14 | Systems research experiments and defense |

## Learning objectives

- Understand the central mathematical objects, algorithms and research methods.
- Identify assumptions, applicability limits and failure conditions.
- Connect literature review, theoretical analysis and reproducible experiments.

## Research assignment

Improve a training or inference bottleneck. Report communication and memory models, correctness and performance across scales, including anomalies and applicability limits.

## Deliverables

Submit a research report with the necessary code or proofs. State the problem, related work, method, evidence and limitations.
