## Overview

Study the probabilistic structure and objectives of deep generative models, connecting variational inference, invertible transformations, autoregressive modeling, energy models, diffusion and flow matching. The second half emphasizes continuity equations, stochastic differential equations and optimal transport.

**Duration**: 14 weeks · Graduate level  
**Prerequisites**: Probability, deep learning, ordinary differential equations and introductory stochastic analysis.

## Learning objectives

- Understand the central mathematical objects, algorithms and research methods.
- Identify assumptions, applicability limits and failure conditions.
- Connect literature review, theoretical analysis and reproducible experiments.

## Research assignment

Compare generation paths, vector-field parameterizations and numerical integrators under a fixed compute budget. Propose an improvement and analyze its mechanism.

## Deliverables

Submit a research report with the necessary code or proofs. State the problem, related work, method, evidence and limitations.
