## Research syllabus

A 14-week research-oriented course. The Chinese edition contains the complete supplied notes. This English outline is an editorial summary.

| Weeks | Research topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | MDP, POMDP, Bellman Operator, Occupancy Measures |
| 3–4 | Stochastic Optimal Control, HJB Equation, Pontryagin Principle, Control as Inference |
| 5–6 | Policy Gradient, Natural Policy Gradient, Mirror Descent, Trust Region |
| 7–8 | Offline RL, Off-policy Evaluation, Distributional RL, Conservative Learning |
| 9–10 | Model-based RL, Latent Dynamics, Dreamer, TD-MPC |
| 11–12 | Exploration, Intrinsic Motivation, Skill Discovery, Hierarchical RL, Meta-RL |
| 13 | Robust & Risk-sensitive RL, Multi-agent RL, Mean-field Games |
| 14 | See Chinese syllabus for the full topic description. |

## Research practice

- State assumptions and identify limitations.
- Connect mathematical arguments with reproducible experiments.
- Propose an original question and defend the evidence.
