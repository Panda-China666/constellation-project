## Overview

Connect reinforcement learning with stochastic optimal control through policy optimization, offline evaluation, model error, exploration and risk-sensitive decision making. Emphasis is placed on derivation, convergence conditions and controlled experiments.

**Duration**: 14 weeks · Graduate level  
**Prerequisites**: Probability, optimization, dynamic programming and introductory reinforcement learning.

## Weekly schedule

| Weeks | Research topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | MDPs, POMDPs, Bellman operators, occupancy measures, dual formulations of dynamic programming |
| 3–4 | Stochastic optimal control, HJB equations, Pontryagin principles, control as inference |
| 5–6 | Policy gradients, natural policy gradients, mirror descent, trust regions, convergence analysis |
| 7–8 | Offline RL, off-policy evaluation, distributional RL, conservative learning |
| 9–10 | Model-based RL, latent dynamics, Dreamer, TD-MPC, model-error propagation |
| 11–12 | Exploration, intrinsic motivation, skill discovery, hierarchical RL, meta-RL |
| 13 | Robust and risk-sensitive RL, multi-agent RL, mean-field games |
| 14 | Research defense: RL theory or algorithmic innovation |

## Learning objectives

- Understand the central mathematical objects, algorithms and research methods.
- Identify assumptions, applicability limits and failure conditions.
- Connect literature review, theoretical analysis and reproducible experiments.

## Research assignment

Formulate a theoretical or algorithmic RL question, analyze its assumptions, and evaluate sample efficiency, stability and generalization in controlled environments.

## Deliverables

Submit a research report with the necessary code or proofs. State the problem, related work, method, evidence and limitations.
