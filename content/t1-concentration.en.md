## Concept

Concentration inequalities control deviations from typical values. The required assumptions may include independence, boundedness or sub-Gaussian tails. For a random variable with finite variance:

$$P(|X-\mathbb{E}X|\ge t)\le \frac{\operatorname{Var}(X)}{t^2},\qquad t>0.$$

## Further study

Develop proofs, examples and exercises alongside a probability textbook, with attention to the assumptions of each result.
