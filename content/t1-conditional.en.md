## Concept

For a sub-sigma-algebra $\mathcal{G}\subseteq\mathcal{F}$ and integrable $X$, conditional expectation is a $\mathcal{G}$-measurable random variable satisfying, for every $A\in\mathcal{G}$:

$$\int_A \mathbb{E}[X\mid\mathcal{G}]\,dP=\int_A X\,dP.$$
