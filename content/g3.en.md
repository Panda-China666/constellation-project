## Overview

Study multimodal representations, spatial and video generation, latent dynamics and action-conditioned world models. Distinguish visual quality from predictive representation and decision value, emphasizing state consistency, physical constraints and long-horizon error.

**Duration**: 14 weeks · Graduate level  
**Prerequisites**: Generative modeling, representation learning, basic geometry and reinforcement learning.

## Weekly schedule

| Weeks | Research topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Multimodal representation learning: contrastive learning, masked modeling, joint embeddings, JEPA |
| 3–4 | Visual and spatial representations: 3D/4D geometry, NeRF, 3D Gaussian splatting, scene representations |
| 5–6 | Video generative models: spatiotemporal attention, video diffusion, flow-based video generation |
| 7–8 | Latent dynamics, state-space world models, action-conditioned prediction |
| 9–10 | Physical world models: physical consistency, object persistence, causal interventions, counterfactuals |
| 11–12 | World models for planning: latent planning, MPC, uncertainty-aware prediction |
| 13 | World-action models, vision-language-action, joint learning of world models and robot policies |
| 14 | World-model research proposal and experimental defense |

## Learning objectives

- Understand the central mathematical objects, algorithms and research methods.
- Identify assumptions, applicability limits and failure conditions.
- Connect literature review, theoretical analysis and reproducible experiments.

## Research assignment

Design a world-model experiment that separately evaluates prediction, action controllability and downstream planning. Analyze how modeling errors affect decisions.

## Deliverables

Submit a research report with the necessary code or proofs. State the problem, related work, method, evidence and limitations.
