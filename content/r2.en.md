## Overview

Study preference learning, verifiable rewards, inference-time computation and long-horizon credit assignment. Analyze how training and inference budgets influence reasoning, including verifier failures, reward exploitation and policy degradation.

**Duration**: 14 weeks · Graduate level  
**Prerequisites**: Reinforcement learning, language-model training and probabilistic optimization; R1 and G2 are recommended.

## Weekly schedule

| Weeks | Research topics |
| --- | --- |

| 3–4 | Reasoning RL: PPO, GRPO, DAPO, advantage estimation, sequence-level rewards |
| 5–6 | RL with verifiable rewards, outcome and process reward models, verifier learning |

| 14 | Independent research defense |

## Learning objectives

- Understand the central mathematical objects, algorithms and research methods.
- Identify assumptions, applicability limits and failure conditions.
- Connect literature review, theoretical analysis and reproducible experiments.

## Research assignment

Design a training mechanism, reward, verification strategy or compute-allocation algorithm. Under matched budgets, report Pass@1, Pass@k, token/FLOP costs and failure cases.

## Deliverables

Submit a research report with the necessary code or proofs. State the problem, related work, method, evidence and limitations.
