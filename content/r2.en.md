## Research syllabus

A 14-week research-oriented course. The Chinese edition contains the complete supplied notes. This English outline is an editorial summary.

| Weeks | Research topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Preference Learning, RLHF, DPO, KL-Regularized Policy Optimization |
| 3–4 | Reasoning RL, PPO, GRPO, DAPO |
| 5–6 | RL with Verifiable Rewards, Outcome/Process Reward Models, Verifier Learning |
| 7–8 | Test-time Scaling, Search, Best-of-N, Tree Search, Adaptive Compute Allocation |
| 9–10 | Long-horizon Credit Assignment, Agentic RL |
| 11 | Self-play, Self-improvement, Synthetic Experience |
| 12 | Diffusion/Flow Policy Optimization, Reward-conditioned Generation |
| 13 | Test-time Training, Online Adaptation, Reward Hacking |
| 14 | Independent Research Defense |

## Research practice

- State assumptions and identify limitations.
- Connect mathematical arguments with reproducible experiments.
- Propose an original question and defend the evidence.
