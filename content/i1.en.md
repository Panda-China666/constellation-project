## Research syllabus

A 14-week research-oriented course. The Chinese edition contains the complete supplied notes. This English outline is an editorial summary.

| Weeks | Research topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Computational Complexity |
| 3–4 | Neural-Symbolic Reasoning, Program Synthesis, Formal Verification, Proof Search |
| 5–6 | Reasoning as Search, Inference-time Computation, Metareasoning |
| 7–8 | Learning to Plan, Belief-State Planning |
| 9–10 | Memory as Learning |
| 11 | Active Information Acquisition, Bayesian Experimental Design |
| 12 | Multi-agent Intelligence |
| 13 | Mechanistic Interpretability, Causal Interventions, Reasoning Faithfulness |
| 14 | Fundamental Research Proposal |

## Research practice

- State assumptions and identify limitations.
- Connect mathematical arguments with reproducible experiments.
- Propose an original question and defend the evidence.
