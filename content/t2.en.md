## Overview

Study neural-network complexity, training dynamics, feature learning and geometry. Compare theoretical frameworks through explicit mathematical objects, with particular attention to singular models and the relationship between infinite-width limits and finite networks.

**Duration**: 14 weeks · Graduate level  
**Prerequisites**: T1 or equivalent mathematics; deep learning and statistical learning theory.

## Weekly schedule

| Weeks | Research topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Singular learning theory: algebraic geometry, singular models, RLCT, Bayesian free energy, local learning coefficients |
| 3–4 | Neural tangent kernels: NNGP, infinite-width limits, NTK dynamics, lazy training |
| 5–6 | Mean-field and feature learning: mean-field PDEs, Wasserstein gradient flow, tensor programs, μP, rich regimes |
| 7–8 | Geometric deep learning: Lie groups, representations, equivariance, manifolds, bundles, gauge equivariance, GNN expressivity |
| 9–10 | Representation geometry: manifolds, alignment, anisotropy, spectra, topology, collapse, identifiability |
| 11–12 | Deep learning dynamics: stochastic SGD dynamics, Hessian spectra, flatness, implicit bias, grokking, scaling laws |
| 13–14 | Open problems: complexity, emergent features, mechanistic interpretability, generalization and model scale |

## Learning objectives

- Understand the central mathematical objects, algorithms and research methods.
- Identify assumptions, applicability limits and failure conditions.
- Connect literature review, theoretical analysis and reproducible experiments.

## Research assignment

Formulate a provable or falsifiable question about NTKs, feature learning, local learning coefficients or representation geometry. Submit a proof, counterexample or controlled experiment.

## Deliverables

Submit a research report with the necessary code or proofs. State the problem, related work, method, evidence and limitations.
